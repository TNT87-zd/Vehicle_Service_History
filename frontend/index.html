Vehicle Service History
Transparent on Blockchain.

Vehicle owners register their vehicles and authorize a mechanic. Each service entry is recorded by a smart contract with a blockchain timestamp. Anyone can look up the service history.

VEHICLE SERVICE HISTORY
Solidity · Ganache · ethers.js
Blockchain—Ganache local
Current block—Transactions recorded on-chain
Role for selected vehicle—Wallet-based permissions

01 · Register Vehicle

Any wallet can register a vehicle identifier that has not been registered yet.

Vehicle IDs are converted to uppercase for consistent lookup.

02 · Authorize Mechanic

Only the vehicle owner can authorize or replace a mechanic.

03 · Add Service Record

Only the currently authorized mechanic can add a service record.

The timestamp is recorded by the blockchain and cannot be edited manually.

04 · Look Up Vehicle

Public lookup by vehicle ID; a wallet connection is not required for reading.

To submit transactions, connect MetaMask and select the authorized account.
